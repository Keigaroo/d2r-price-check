D2R PRICE CHECK
===============
Hover an item in Diablo II: Resurrected, press Ctrl+D, and a window next to your
mouse shows what similar items recently sold for on Traderie, priced in runes.

Needs: Windows 10 or 11, internet connection.
Prices are for: PC, Softcore, Ladder (see "Changing the market" below).


INSTALL (one time, about 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------
1. Right-click "D2RPriceCheck.zip" and choose "Extract All...".
2. In the box that opens, change the folder to:   C:\D2RPriceCheck
   then click "Extract".
3. Open the C:\D2RPriceCheck folder and double-click "Setup.bat".
   - If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears: click "More info",
     then "Run anyway". (It appears because the file isn't from a big company.)
   - If Python isn't installed, Setup installs it for you.
   - Wait until the window says "Setup finished" and shows SELFTEST PASSED,
     then press any key to close it.
   - Setup puts a "D2R Price Check" shortcut on your desktop.


USE
---
1. Double-click "D2R Price Check" on your desktop. A black window opens and says
   "D2R Price Check is running". Leave it open while you play.
2. In D2R, hover your mouse over an item so its tooltip shows.
3. Press Ctrl+D. The result appears next to your mouse after a few seconds.
4. The result closes when you move the mouse away, or press Ctrl+D again.
5. To quit, close the black window.

If nothing happens when you press Ctrl+D: close the black window, right-click the
desktop shortcut, choose "Run as administrator", and try again.
If the result doesn't show over the game: press Alt+Enter in D2R (windowed mode).


READING THE RESULT
------------------
Estimated value  - weighted middle price of recent sales of similar items
Typical range    - where most of those sales landed
Confidence       - HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW: how much matching trade data there was
Most similar trades - the actual trades the estimate is based on
Buttons: Traderie (opens the item's page), diablo2.io and d2jsp (copy the item
name and open that site so you can paste it with Ctrl+V).


CHANGING THE MARKET (non-ladder, hardcore, console)
---------------------------------------------------
After the first run, open C:\D2RPriceCheck\config.json in Notepad and change:
   "mode": "softcore"    ->  "hardcore"
   "ladder": true        ->  false        (for non-ladder)
   "platform": "PC"      ->  "playstation", "xbox" or "switch"
   "hotkey": "ctrl+d"    ->  any other key combination
Save, close the black window, and start the program again.


UPDATES
-------
Every time you start it, it checks for a newer version and installs it automatically
(you'll see "Updating D2R Price Check ..." in the black window). Nothing to do.


WHAT IT DOES AND DOESN'T DO
---------------------------
It only takes a screenshot of the area around your mouse and reads the text.
It never reads game memory and never presses keys in the game.
Prices come from Traderie's public trade data (latest 50 trades per item).
